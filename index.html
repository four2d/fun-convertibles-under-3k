Rust & expensive faults

Buyer's guides

Mazda MX-5 NC / NB

Rust risk: medium
Rust
Rear subframe, rear arches and sill ends; NB sills and chassis rails. Underseal can hide rot.
Expensive faults
Engine mostly bulletproof; timing-chain tensioner, seizing calipers, rear diff leaks, cracking coolant tank.
Parts & repairs
Cheap, plentiful parts; huge specialist and club network.

Sources: Autocar · Parkers · CarThrottle · MX5OC forum

BMW Z4 E85

Rust risk: low
Rust
Body resists rot well; rear brake lines corrode; occasional rear-arch blisters.
Expensive faults
Roof motor flooded by a blocked drain (dealer ~€1,200–1,500, specialist €200–400); cooling plastics (expansion tank, thermostat, water pump).
Parts & repairs
Independent BMW specialists make it affordable; parts plentiful.

Sources: Classics World · Parkers · carchecker.pro

BMW Z3 1.9

Rust risk: low
Rust
'Not as much of a rust trap as a contemporary MX5 or MG TF'; check arches, sills, rear suspension mounts, boot floor.
Expensive faults
Simple and robust; cooling plastics – an overheat can turn a pump job into a head gasket; timing-chain rattle, oil leaks.
Parts & repairs
Simple E30-era running gear, cheap to fix.

Sources: Parkers · carchecker.pro

MINI Convertible R52/R57

Rust risk: low
Rust
Not flagged as a major issue in our sources; check for water ingress via roof drains and seals.
Expensive faults
R52 CVT auto fails (avoid); supercharged R52 power-steering pump; pre-Nov 2011 Prince timing-chain tensioners; roof hydraulics £395–495 average repair, R57 roof from ~£1,735.
Parts & repairs
Plentiful parts; roof specialists exist.

Sources: mini-trader · AustralianCar.Reviews R52 · AustralianCar.Reviews R57

MG MGF / TF

Rust risk: high
Rust
Can be serious underneath; mild-steel underfloor coolant pipes rust; radiators corrode.
Expensive faults
K-series head gasket – want proof of MLS gasket, revised oil rail, replaced dowels; VVC mechanism; Hydragas suspension on MGF.
Parts & repairs
Cheap parts, strong club scene.

Sources: Parkers · Hagerty · Classics World

Toyota MR2 Roadster

Rust risk: medium
Rust
Body rot not a major concern, but the rear subframe rots.
Expensive faults
Pre-cat breakdown scores the bores (pre-2003 worst) – compression test, look for blue smoke; many have pre-cats removed.
Parts & repairs
Durable 1ZZ engine if well maintained.

Sources: Classic & Sports Car · Octane

Smart Roadster 452

Rust risk: medium
Rust
Galvanised Tridion cell; check underbody, subframe and rear arches.
Expensive faults
Chronic water leaks reaching the SAM module (replacement £400+); turbo failures around 70–90k miles; jerky automated gearbox.
Parts & repairs
Specialists exist, but it's a fair-weather second car.

Sources: Autocar · Evilution · Smart Car Manuals

Audi TT Roadster (8N/8J)

Rust risk: medium
Rust
Mostly OK, but Mk2 rear steel section and the metalwork around the hood can corrode; blocked hood drains soak carpets.
Expensive faults
Mk1: timing belt/water pump, coilpacks, failing dash pod (~£200). Mk2 2.0 TFSI: cam follower (2006-08), oil use and chain tensioner (2009-11); Haldex service on quattros.
Parts & repairs
Huge VAG parts supply; dashpod specialists.

Sources: Classics World · Classic & Sports Car · TT Forum Mk2 Roadster · Audi Breaker Yards

Mercedes SLK R170/R171

Rust risk: medium
Rust
R171 (2004-11) galvanised and largely rust-free, but rear subframes corrode (MOT fails). R170 rusts badly – wings, arches, sills, screen pillars.
Expensive faults
Vario roof: hydraulic ram leaks, microswitches, should cycle in ~25-30s; M272 V6 balance-shaft gear on early cars; cracked exhaust Y-pipe; auto box wants oil changes.
Parts & repairs
Specialists and SLK club; roof parts available but labour adds up.

Sources: Autocar · Classics World R170 · SLK World

VW Eos

Rust risk: low
Rust
Body generally sound; the hidden risk is water pooling at the roof pump next to the spare wheel and corroding it.
Expensive faults
Roof leaks into boot/footwells (early cars worst), jammed roof mechanism, electrical gremlins; a crash-damaged roof can cost a fortune.
Parts & repairs
Golf parts underneath are cheap; roof expertise is scarce.

Sources: What Car? · AutoTrader.ca · VW Eos Club

How we ranked

Then a fun-factor credit (MX-5 highest, then Z4, MR2 and TT), a known-fault risk penalty (MG head gasket, Smart leaks, MINI drivetrain, Eos roof), a credit for full service history, a credit for an MOT running into 2027 and a penalty for an MOT that has run out (MOT dates from the AutoTrader ad where given). The list is capped at 50: each day sold cars come out and a new find goes in only if it beats the weakest car. It's a rough guide, not gospel. Distances are the ones AutoTrader shows for GU7 1AA.
